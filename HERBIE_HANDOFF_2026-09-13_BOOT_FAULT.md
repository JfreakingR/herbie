## THE BLOCKING FAULT: Android never boots

Herbie's Android SoC boot-loops at the MediaTek preloader and never reaches
Android. Over USB, Windows enumerates `MT65xx Preloader`
(`USB\VID_0E8D&PID_2000`, status Error / no driver), holds ~7-10 s, drops, and
retries.

| Observation window | Cycle period | Up-window |
|---|---|---|
| ~16:45 | ~38 s, metronomic | exactly 7 s |
| ~17:35 | ~50 s | 7-10 s |
| ~17:44 | ~113 s to first attempt | 7 s |

The 7-second up-window is a fixed firmware timeout: the preloader waits for a
flash-tool handshake, gets none, attempts to boot Android, fails, resets.

**Consequences:** no ADB over USB or Wi-Fi; he never joins Wi-Fi
(`192.168.1.122` dead, MAC `<HERBIE_WLAN_MAC>` absent from a full
`192.168.1.0/24` sweep, repeated). Network sweeps are pointless until he boots.

### Ruled out

- **The frozen factory app is NOT the cause.** The `kill -STOP` on
  `com.sego.toy.ctl`/`.daem` is userspace, long after the preloader. The
  "reboot to restore it" step in the 09-09 handoff is moot.
- **Not the USB cable or PC port** — the Galaxy enumerates fine on the same
  cable and port (`R5CR11QCHPY`, authorized).
- **Not the barrel tip** — swapping it changed nothing; the original is back on.
- **Not the factory reset** — the loop predates it and is unchanged by it.

### Leading suspect: the missing battery

Three symptoms, one explanation:

| Symptom | Fits missing battery? |
|---|---|
| MTK preloader loop | Yes — preloaders commonly abort without pack voltage |
| Repeating alarm beeping (new, see below) | Yes — `LOW_VOLTAGE` is a real alarm here |
| Erratic charging indicator | Yes — charger sees no cell |

DEX strings confirm the hardware has first-class voltage alarms:
`LOW_VOLTAGE`, `VOLTAGE_LOW`, `LI_ALARM_CONDITION`, `cellVoltage`,
`mainVoltage`, plus `beep`. `KEY_LOW_VOLTAGE = 3` was already known as a key
event.

**Unresolved contradiction:** `HARDWARE_INVENTORY.md:29` records a successful
battery-less boot on 2026-09-09 with this same adapter, and the owner states it
"worked every other time". That is not explained. If a battery does not fix it,
the next suspect is internal — he has been opened before (see
`PAL_HANDOFF_2026-09-07_RIBBON_REASSEMBLY.md`), and "worked every other time,
then didn't" fits a connector loosened by handling.

### The measurement that was never taken

**Meter the barrel while he cycles** — red to center, black to sleeve, across a
full cycle. Steady ~16.8 V exonerates the supply; a dip indicts it. The
adapter's 2 A rating passes "on paper only" (`HARDWARE_INVENTORY.md:113`) and
has never been measured under load. The stretching retry interval is weakly
consistent with a marginal supply. **Do this before buying anything.**

## NEW: Herbie moved for the first time

Unprompted, he drove **back and forth and beeped** — while boot-looping, with
no Android, no ADB, no factory app, and no serial command from us.

Beep pattern as heard: `beep beep ......... beep beep beep beeep .....`

**This is the most important finding of the session**, for three reasons:

1. **Movement does not require Android or the serial protocol.** The STM32
   drives the motors natively and has autonomous behaviours we have never
   characterised. Every prior session assumed motion had to come through
   `/dev/ttyMT1`.
2. **The 8-second factory reset DID register — at the STM32**, not in Android.
   Back-and-forth plus beeping is characteristic of a self-test, setup dance, or
   error indication.
3. It happened **on adapter power**, which is the basis of the correction below.

Beep codes are **not decodable from anything we hold**: they are generated by
STM32 firmware that has never been dumped, the manual documents no beep codes
(verified by search), and the Android APK cannot contain them.

## Do not

- **Do not write to the MTK preloader.** It is the flash-tool interface and
  there is no firmware backup for this board. Diagnosis only.
- **Do not connect J21 `RX` or `3.3V`** — receive-only keeps the ESP32 unable to
  command the STM32.
- **Do not re-introduce an ESP32-owns-motors design.** Standing owner
  instruction: motor control belongs to the brain. The listener is passive and
  does not violate this.
- **Do not retry BLE/phone Wi-Fi provisioning** — documented dead end across two
  sessions; the vendor cloud is dead (`IOTC_Device_Login -1`).
- **Do not trust `/sys/class/power_supply/ac/online`** (reads 0 with adapter in);
  use `dumpsys battery` / `mPlugType=2`.
- **Do not use Herbie's LEDs to diagnose Android state** — they are driven by the
  STM32 and charging circuit and stay steady through the entire boot loop.
